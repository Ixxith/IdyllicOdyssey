A stone giant lich, who once ruled the lands of the [[Kingdom of Nilt]]. He originally was a peaceful king, living in the lands after the Giantfolk were pushed out of their original lands by the dragons. He came to fall in love with Isabelle, a human woman. 

At some point, the dragons and Giantfolk came into conflict. During a raid, Isabelle was slain. Enraged, Grugaran led a massive army of Giants and their allies against the dragons. While victorious in the battle, most of the Giant forces were lost. After the battle, he met a devil who offered a way to bring back Isabelle. 

However the price was that Grugaran was turned into a lich, and what little empathy he had left was completely gone. He began to enslave all non-giants or giantkin, and began a tyrannical reign where he attempted to rebuild

However, rebel forces arose to stop Grugaran, and he was defeated by the human hero Ria. Unknown to most people, she was unable to defeat Grugaran, not knowing where his phylactery was located, and instead imprisoned him in a jade statue, hiding it.

A cult known as the [[Grugarians]] are attempting to resurrect this lich and have stolen the jade statue that had the *Imprisonment* spell cast on it.
