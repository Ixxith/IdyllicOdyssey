An extremely tall and lanky Goliath. His full name is Fodurlauss, which he hates as it means "fatherless" in Giant.

The party observed him doing an obvious job of stalking the party while visiting [[Virtue]]. They led him to an obscure part of the park before ambushing him and forcing him to answer questions. Luckily for the party, Fodur was a terrible spy and spilled the beans that he was a part of the [[Grugarians]], and had been tasked with following them.

He made a small acquaintance with the party, following them to the Public Pool, but while the party was patrolling the sewers, he was handed an urn full of remnants of [[Grugaran the Dodkong]], which instantly took possession of his body, turning him into a bone golem.
