A large and ancient forest. Rumors speak of it containing a trap made by [[Grugaran the Dodkong]] himself, to trap the hero Ria as she approached with the rebel army.

The party discovered this trap, while transporting an ancient cup to [[Sheerla]], finding themselves outside of time and space, in the forest. As they travelled, they travelled deeper, encountering long extinct monsters, and ancient people who told them of their time.

As they travelled, they realized that the trap was meant to trap those who were transporting something of importance. By throwing the item away, they were able to escape the trap.
