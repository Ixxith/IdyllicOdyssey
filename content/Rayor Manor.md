Where [[Bibfius Tirog Rayor]] resides in [[Sheerla]]. A large and spacious manor. The bottom floor has been converted into a sort of museum, with many glass displays, each item having flawless handwriting annotating the item and where it was found.

While the party was leaving, they realized a [[Mysterious Woman]] was following them. They managed to apprehend her and turned her over to the police.
