A group of adventurers that the party met in [[Rovum]]. Most of them are quite obnoxious.

Members
- Hansor Hascolia - son of Lord Edwilissya Hascolia (lord of [[Rovum]]). Human paladin. Generally friendly, but has a habit of being self-righteous and downplaying the antics of his party members.
- Edgar Lightfinger - Kleptomaniac halfing rogue that flirts with anything with a pulse.
- Gel Gordih - Orc barbarian. Has a bad temper and tries to fight with everyone. 
- Carrin Aelith - Half-elf sorceror. Stuck-up and snobby. Part of the nobility, of the Aelith family.
- The Silent One - Unknown race. Warlock who tries to give off a dangerous aura with their glowing eyes and lack of speech.
