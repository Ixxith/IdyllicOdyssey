Town in [[Kingdom of Nilt]]. Ruled by the local lord of Lord Edwilissya Hascolia. This village is well known for its fishing. The town is broken up into five "quarters":
- Quarry Quarter - the poorest of the quarter, once a quarry, now most laborers live here.
- Farmer's Quarter - mostly used for storage and processing of the outlying farms in the area.
- Merchant's Quarter - shops, ships, and crafting districts. Most inns are found here, such as the Singing Maiden and the Sleeping Puppy.
- Middle Quarter - most middle class residents live here.
- Upper Quarter - where the richest of the rich live. The Lord Hascolia lives here with her family.

Here the party found employ by [[Bibfius Tirog Rayor]], after recovering a lost ring for him in the sewer and seeing a vision of [[Grugaran the Dodkong]]. They completed several quests for the town to earn a better reputation, such as taking guard over an old artifact that summoned ice spikes, helping an old lady appease the local fish spirits, and helping a fisherman win the fishing competition.

Here they also met their competing party, the [[Glorious Hunters]].
