A mysterious woman who has caught following the party after they left [[Rayor Manor]]. She seems to be a spellcaster of some kind, and after the party apprehended her, they removed her mask, revealing her to be a young elf, though she did not give her name.

Some time later, she escaped from police custody, and was blamed for the murder on the clock tower of the Gnomish shopkeeper.
