A village in the [[Kingdom of Nilt]].

A small river breaks up the village, with most residents living on the east side, either in the village proper itself, or a few miles away, with their own fields and livestock. All sorts of peoples can be found living in the village, some lifelong residents, others retired adventurers, and some with dark pasts that they hope to leave behind.   

The party were all residents of the small village of Stinstag. For whatever reason, they have spent most of their life growing up here, either working the fields, mending equipment, or hiding in their small home reading books about the great lands beyond. However, recently, for personal reasons, the party members decided this village was not the fit for them. They were ready to become an adventurer, to have epic quests. Each party member normally would have done this on their own, but luckily some of their friends and acquaintances in the village have expressed similar interest, forming a proper adventurer’s party.

Together, with their meager supplies and powers, the party members have diligently prepared to leave their  small hometown. The summer night before, Stinstag held a grand feast in honor of the party, with plenty of food and drink. They spoke with Chief Melvin and got some final advice before they left, and sent them to help the farmer, [[Arther Andersen]].
